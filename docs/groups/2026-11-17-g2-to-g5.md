Hi Group 5, thanks for the detailed review. Here are our answers to your questions, and two questions back.

1. **promoterId.** Yes. We key the Promoter by the user id, so promoterId is the id the Gateway sends as X-User-Id, in the Booking request and in all our messages. We keep the name promoterId.
2. **Rebooking.** Yes. We use replacesBookingId for postponeEvent, for updateEvent with a new place and for bookPlace on a draft that already has a place. We read the Venue and check its capacity before the request, and after a 201 we save again rather than cancel the new Booking. One detail: when a postponement keeps the same days and only changes the time, we don't send a Rebooking.
3. **Exchange.** Our exchange is called events and the routing key of each message is its type. The spelling is EventCancelled, with two l's; the Collaborations table had a typo, now fixed.
4. **Calls and conventions.** Yes. We call you directly with X-Service-Name: events and adopt your conventions. We send firstDay and lastDay as the Europe/Lisbon dates of the Event's startDateTime and endDateTime. Your 409 and 422 answers go to the Promoter as business errors.
5. **Paths.** Yes. We are removing /venues/{id}/rating, since your API already returns the Rating, and moving review-eligibility out of /venues (to /events/review-eligibility, unless Reviews agrees to drop it). GET /places/{id} is now GET /venues/{venueId} in our report.
6. **Venue Manager cancellations.** Yes, we consume BookingCancelled, matched by bookingId, and ignore those with cancelledBy: events. [A draft Event loses its Venue and stays a draft. A published Event is cancelled automatically: we publish EventCancelled, so ticket holders are refunded.]
7. **Naming.** Agreed, Room_request becomes Booking request on the shared diagram.

We also accept your points on PlaceUpdated (we refresh our copy only through GET /venues/{venueId}) and on Ratings in the search.

Our questions:

a) **Recovery after a 201.** If our service fails between your 201 and saving our Event, we have no way to learn the new Booking's id: repeating the request gives unknown-booking or event-already booked. Could you offer GET /bookings?eventId={eventId} returning the Event's current Booking, or accept an idempotency key on POST /bookings?

b) **EventCancelled with no Booking.** [When the Venue Manager cancels a Booking, we cancel the Event and publish EventCancelled.] You will then receive EventCancelled for an Event whose Booking is already gone. Can you confirm you ignore it without error? The same happens for an Event cancelled after its Booking ended.
