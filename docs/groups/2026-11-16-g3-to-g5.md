# Group 3 (Tickets and Billing) → Group 5 (Places)
 
## 1. What we need from you
 
**`BookingConfirmed`** (from your report, section 4.2.1)
 
Billing creates a charge for the promoter, for the booking's amount: one charge per `bookingId`.
Fields we use: `bookingId`, `eventId`, `promoterId`, `venueId`, `firstDay`, `lastDay`, `amount`.
 
**`BookingCancelled`**
 
If the promoter has not paid the charge yet, Billing cancels it.
Fields we use: `bookingId`.
 
**`GET /venues/{venueId}`**
 
Ticketing reads the venue's `name` and `address` (`street`, `postalCode`, `city`) to print them on tickets. We call it once per event, when Group 2 publishes the event, directly (not through the Gateway) with `X-Service-Name: tickets`. We rely on it also returning unlisted venues, as your FR-10 says.
 
## 2. What we send you
 
Nothing. You don't need to consume any of our messages or call our API.
