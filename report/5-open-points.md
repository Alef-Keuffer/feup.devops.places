# Open points

Delivery 1 leaves some points open, and this section collects them: what Billing's choice on refunds means for Promoters, what delivery 1 leaves out, and the questions for the professor and the other groups. Wiegers and Beatty advise marking each piece of information that is still missing, rather than leaving the gap unstated, and resolving it before the requirements it affects are implemented [@wiegers2013, ch. 10]. For each question, this section gives the position the report takes until the answer comes.

## Paid bills are not refunded

Billing (G3) creates the bill from *Booking confirmed*, and cancels it on *Booking cancelled* if the Promoter hasn't paid it yet. A paid bill is not refunded, whoever cancelled the Booking; G3 has told us this is deliberate for delivery 1. It matters most when a Venue Manager cancels after the Promoter has paid: the Promoter has lost both the Venue and the amount. Places can't change this on its own, because bills belong to Billing (Table \ref{tab:ownership}). A Rebooking is different: Billing carries a paid bill over to the Booking that replaces it, so a Promoter who postpones doesn't pay twice.

## Out of scope for delivery 1

Delivery 1 leaves out Venue approval, transferring a Venue to another Venue Manager, hourly or seasonal pricing, distance search, keyword and equipment filters, blocking days outside Bookings, and refunds and cancellation fees. The Scope in the Application requirements section says where each stands. One of them is open rather than settled: blocking days waits on question L1 below. Refunds are Billing's to decide, and the section above gives its choice.
